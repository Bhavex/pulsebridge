Why this matters

Help can feel closer, even before it arrives.

Finding a donor should not mean trading away someone’s privacy. PulseBridge gives receivers a focused way to search by blood group and place, then sends a respectful contact request to a human admin.

For donors, that means your phone number and email stay in a protected collection. For receivers, it means a clearer next step when the clock feels loud.

The PulseBridge path

Three simple steps. One thoughtful handoff.

01

Search the signal

Filter potential donors by blood group, city, and state from a directory built for clarity.

02

Send a request

Share your name, phone, urgency, and a short message—never ask a donor to publish their details.

03

Let people bridge it

The admin team sees the request and the protected donor contact details, then follows up manually.

Please keep in mind

PulseBridge connects people. Professionals guide care.

Blood compatibility, donation eligibility, and treatment decisions must always be confirmed by qualified medical professionals. Donors must be 18+ and must have consented to being listed.
