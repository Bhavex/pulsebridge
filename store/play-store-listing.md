## Full description
PulseBridge helps families find willing blood donors quickly, without turning donors' phone numbers into a public list.

NEED BLOOD?
• Search donors by blood group, city, state and nearby hospital
• Include compatible blood groups to find more donors
• Sort by hospital, city, best match or most recently confirmed
• Send a request with the hospital's blood requisition slip
• When the donor accepts, call or WhatsApp them in one tap

WANT TO DONATE?
• Register in a minute with your blood group, city and nearest hospital
• Your phone number is never shown in search
• See every request with its hospital slip and decide yourself
• Accept, decline, or report anything that looks fake
• Pause or delete your profile any time

BUILT FOR TRUST AND PRIVACY
• Numbers are shared only between a donor and a recipient after the donor accepts
• Monthly availability check, so you only see donors who can actually help
• Rate limits and reporting keep scammers out
• Available in English, हिन्दी, বাংলা, मराठी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், ગુજરાતી and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ

PulseBridge is free. Blood donation in India is voluntary — never pay or accept money for blood. PulseBridge is not an emergency service: in an emergency, call 108 or go to the nearest hospital. Blood compatibility and donor eligibility must always be confirmed by the hospital or blood bank.

## Data safety form answers
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Users can request deletion (donors delete their profile in the app).

| Data type | Collected | Shared with third parties | Purpose | Optional |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | No (shown to a matched person only after the donor accepts) | App functionality | No |
| Email address | Yes (Google sign-in) | No | Account management | No |
| Phone number | Yes | No (shown to a matched person only after the donor accepts) | App functionality | No |
| Approximate location (city, state) | Yes, typed by user | No | App functionality | No |
| Health info (blood group, last donation date) | Yes | No | App functionality | Last donation date is optional |
| Photos / files (hospital requisition slip) | Yes | No | App functionality, fraud prevention | Required to send a request |
| App diagnostics (error messages) | Yes | No | Analytics / app functionality | No |

No data is sold. No advertising. No tracking across other apps.
