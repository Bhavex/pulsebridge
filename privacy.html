Clear by design

Privacy policy.

PulseBridge helps people connect without turning a donor list into a public contact list. This plain-language summary explains what we collect and who can see it.

Version 2.0 · Review date: to be filled in before launch.
01

What we collect

Sign-in uses Google through Supabase Authentication, which gives us your Google name, email and a user ID. Donors give us their name, age, blood group, phone number, city, state, a nearby hospital, and optionally their last donation date and donation registration number. Recipients give us the patient's name, blood group needed, units, hospital, required-by date, their own name and phone, an optional message, and a photo or PDF of the hospital's blood requisition slip.

02

What appears in search

Search shows only a donor's first name and last initial, age, blood group, city, state, nearby hospital, whether they are eligible to donate now, and roughly when they last donated. Phone numbers, emails, full names and registration numbers never appear in search. Search is limited to signed-in users and is rate-limited to stop copying of the list.

03

When phone numbers are shared

A donor's phone number is shared with a recipient only after that donor accepts that recipient's request, and the recipient's number is shared with the donor at the same time. Each time a number is viewed it is logged. The hospital slip is visible only to the recipient who uploaded it, the donor it was sent to while the request is open, and PulseBridge admins.

04

Monthly availability check

Every 30 days we email donors a one-tap link to confirm they are still available. Donors who don't confirm within 37 days are hidden from search until they confirm again.

05

Your choices

Donors can edit their details, pause their availability, or permanently delete their profile from the donor page at any time. Recipients can cancel requests. To have any other data removed, contact the PulseBridge team (contact email to be added before launch).

06

Safety

Donors can report fake requests, and admins can block accounts that misuse PulseBridge. Never pay or accept money for blood. If someone asks for money, report the request.

07

Medical disclaimer

PulseBridge is not a hospital, blood bank, clinician or emergency service. Compatibility, donation eligibility and treatment must be confirmed by qualified medical professionals. In an emergency, call 108 or go to the nearest hospital.
