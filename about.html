Why PulseBridge exists

People are already willing to help. The path should honor that.

PulseBridge is a donor-finder designed around a simple belief: urgency does not require abandoning dignity or privacy.

Human-first by default
01

Clarity for receivers.

When someone needs blood, searching can feel like a second emergency. PulseBridge narrows the first step to the information that actually helps: blood group and location.

A Google sign-in keeps search accountable. Every request carries the hospital's requisition slip, so donors can trust what they're answering.

02

Privacy for donors.

A donor’s generosity should not make their phone number public. PulseBridge never shows phone numbers in search. A donor's number is shared only with a person whose request they accept.

Donors register themselves, give clear consent, confirm their availability every month, and can pause or delete their profile at any time.
