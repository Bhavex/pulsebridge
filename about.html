Why PulseBridge exists

People are already willing to help. The path should honor that.

PulseBridge is a donor-finder designed around a simple belief: urgency does not require abandoning dignity or privacy.

Human-first by default
01

Clarity for receivers.

When someone needs blood, searching can feel like a second emergency. PulseBridge narrows the first step to the information that actually helps: blood group and location.

There is no separate registration maze. A Google sign-in keeps the search accountable, then a short contact request gives an admin enough context to follow up.

02

Privacy for donors.

A donor’s generosity should not make their phone number public. PulseBridge stores public directory details separately from contact details, and only the configured admin can see the protected record.

Every donor record requires a clear consent confirmation and an age of at least 18 before it can be saved.
